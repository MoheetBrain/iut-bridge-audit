# Exact source target and typing boundary

## Source statement first

**SOURCE FACT [C003]:** LANA §9.2, PDF p.46, equation (9-1), asks for a suitable choice of S such that

\[
\eta^{q}=\eta^{\mathrm{anab}}_{S}. \tag{9-1}
\]

**SOURCE CLAIM [C001–C002; R_O_RVAL; R_O_RSS]:** The input is a BPS \(\mathcal B=(\{G_v\curvearrowright M_v\},C)\), with \(\mathbb R^{\mathrm{val}}=C\). The report displays

\[
\mathbb R^{\mathrm{ss}}
 =\{T\subset VC(\mathcal I_v)\mid T\text{ adelic and measurable}\}/\sim,
\qquad T\sim T'\ \text{if their volumes agree}.
\]

Its anabelian map \(\eta^{\mathrm{anab}}_S:\mathbb R^{\mathrm{val}}\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathbb R^{\mathrm{ss}}\) is described as an isomorphism of pointed real vector spaces. The target pilot is \([M]\), where \(M=1\cdot S\) for the reconstructed discrete \(\mathbb N\)-action, equivalently the LGP action. Here `1·S` is the action of the discrete generator, not multiplication of a vector by the real scalar 1.

The map \(\eta^q\) has the same displayed domain and codomain, using the q-BPS of the native arithmetic-holomorphic structure. Its target pilot is the class of \(\{q_v\mathcal O_v\}\), embedded diagonally in the large container. The report does not expand that diagonal or the later shorthand q versus its earlier chosen \(2\ell\)-th-root normalization [§4.2(c), p.26; §9.2, p.46; R_U07].

## Meaning of the structures

**SOURCE FACT [C012, C015]:** LANA Definition 4.1.3 and Remark 4.1.5, pp.23–24, use an oriented one-dimensional real vector space with a distinguished **nonzero pilot**, the sum of local pilots at the selected bad places. The simplified description chooses the pilot negative in that orientation. Pointed does not merely mean preserving zero. Value-BPS isomorphisms are real-linear and preserve the specified local pilots. The étale-unit portion also retains compact submodules at open subgroups (Definitions 4.1.2, 4.1.6).

**OPEN QUESTION [C017; R_U06–R_U07]:** A precise faithful type for \(\mathbb R^{\mathrm{ss}}\) has not been completed. One must specify the accepted regions, positivity/finiteness of volumes, global restricted-product conditions, weights, and vector operations. The displayed quotient of measurable regions alone does not provide all of that. IUT3 Remark 3.1.1(iii)–(iv), pp.95–96, specifically warns that its weighted log-volume is generally not an ordinary Haar-measure logarithm on arbitrary regions [C016]. The pilot sign, degree/log-volume convention and precise q-root must also be reconciled. No contradiction is inferred.

## Fixed initial data

**RECONSTRUCTION [C026]:** Freeze D as the **entire** initial Θ-data of IUT1 Definition 3.1(a)–(f), pp.61–63. This includes the algebraic closure over F (with \(\sqrt{-1}\in F\)); the once-punctured elliptic curve and stable-reduction/field-of-moduli/torsion conditions; the prime \(\ell\ge5\), Galois-image and coprimality conditions; the prescribed theta covering; the section of places over the field of moduli and its bad-place local types; and the distinguished cusp with its local generator compatibility. This is a reference to the full definition, not a claim that the synopsis supplies all covering constructions. DH Definition 2.4.1, p.6, treats only preliminary data. Changing D is not an admissible change within the fixed-D target.

## Normalized statement — still a specification

**RECONSTRUCTION [C003, C018]:** For fixed genuine D, the source target can be written

\[
\boxed{\exists\,S\ \text{suitable in the source sense},\quad
       \eta^q_D=\eta^{\mathrm{anab}}_{D,S}.}\qquad\text{TARGET ★}
\]

“Suitable” is not yet a fully reconstructed predicate. It must be defined independently of the desired equality. Earlier realization and synchronization choices are suppressed by the source notation. The audit therefore keeps a provisional execution record e as well as S; it does **not** assume that all dependence factors through S or that the choices are independent.

A possible completed typing would have a fixed line L_D, source-forced transports \(\tau_{e,S}:L_{e,S}\to L_D\), and the equation

\[
\exists(e,S)\in\mathrm{Adm}(D),\qquad
 \tau_{e,S}\circ\eta^{\mathrm{anab}}_{e,S}=\eta^q_D
 \quad\text{in }\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb R}(C_D,L_D).
\]

This is **conditional bookkeeping**, not an established substitute theorem. The transports are obligations, not arbitrary isomorphisms selected to make equality true. `Adm_partial(D)` in Stage 3 records the unfilled obligations. It is not a defined full mathematical domain.

## What equality would mean

**SOURCE FACT / OPEN QUESTION [C003; R_U07]:** The source writes equality of its two displayed maps, not equality of their abstract isomorphism types. It does not provide, in this compressed passage, every identification needed to certify literal equality of functions in a single fixed Hom-set. No weaker relation or natural-isomorphism equality is silently substituted here.

**INFERENCE [C023]:** If that common one-dimensional linear-map type and its transports are established, equality of the two linear maps is equivalent to equality on the fixed nonzero source pilot. This does not make TARGET ★ tautological: the target point \([1\cdot S]\) varies with S, and its equality with the native q point is precisely unproved. Nor does equality of one arbitrary scalar summary certify equality before the required structural forgetting.

**SOURCE CLAIM [C004, C009, C014]:** LANA pp.43–46 links its goal to Corollary 3.12 but does not prove a source-faithful equivalence of all formulations. FORM pp.10–11 describes the fourth triangle and the simultaneous q/Θ comparison in one ring structure. IUT3 cQ3/cQ4 and xi-d–f retain additional local/global and unit/Galois data. That correspondence is an explicit unresolved obligation, not a source claim discarded by the audit.
