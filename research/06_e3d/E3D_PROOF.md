## STEP 1. Fix Q without reconstructing it from P

**Statement.** The actual codomain column supplies a native marked realization Q and its q-pilot L_q.

**Source.** II Definition 4.9(i)–(viii), pp.153–158; Corollary 4.10(i), pp.158–159; I Definition 5.2(iv), pp.134–135; I Example 3.2(iv), p.71; III Definition 3.8(i)–(iii), pp.112–114.

**Construction.** Take the constant strip at (1,0), including the identification of labels 0 and ⟨Fℓ*⟩ from Corollary 4.10(i). Apply Definition 4.9 and mark the resulting full strip by ι from P. The bad-place splitting is generated by q̲_v modulo the stipulated torsion. Its generators and the localization data specify L_q up to isomorphism. Keep the actual column beside the strip; do not try to invert its forgetful construction.

**Why hypotheses apply.** The two columns belong to the single LGP-Gaussian lattice with the fixed initial Θ-data of Theorem 3.11. The native constant strip is therefore one of the strips to which II Corollary 4.10 applies. The bad set is nonempty; the root and torsion conventions are those of this initial data. The native pilot has negative arithmetic degree by II Definition 4.9(viii). The archimedean data are the cofinal torsion-quotient systems of (v), not a chosen sign or a single complex-unit representative.

**Resulting typed datum.** Q, the maps s_q,v, L_q and its native localization data. No missing type for Q is found at this level of source-defined Hodge-theater data; its full internal construction is accepted as given initial input.

## STEP 2. Construct IPL on full strips

**Statement.** The actual IPL path gives a family of full-strip isomorphisms, not a canonical arithmetic-holomorphic identification.

**Source.** III Remark 3.10.2, pp.151–152; II Corollary 4.10(ii), p.159; III Propositions 2.1(ii), 3.7(iii),(iv), 3.10(i); III Remark 3.11.1(iii), pp.160–161.

**Construction.** For θ:F_LGP^(0,0)≅P_q, compose

`g = u₃ u₂ u₁ k_env b₁⁻¹ b₂⁻¹ θ⁻¹ : P_q → F_lgp^(0,◦)`.

Use the whole allowed family of θ and auxiliary representatives. Each inverted b_i is an isomorphism. In each square `u_i k_i = k_(i+1) b_i`. There is no inversion of a logarithm, inclusion, hull operation or evaluation on arbitrary regions. The induced strip-level evaluation maps here really are isomorphisms.

**Why hypotheses apply.** env/gau and LGP/lgp are the constructions belonging to the same (0,0) theater and its preceding log-link. Proposition 3.10(i) constructs their vertically coric versions using the associated D-Hodge theater and full log-links, with the specified Fℓ-symmetries. These are exactly the objects used in Remark 3.10.2. Theorem 3.11(iii)(c),(d) is accepted for its stated Kummer/evaluation equivariance, including Ind1–3; its hypotheses are the fixed lattice and the associated processions, not the desired native-q specialization.

**Resulting typed datum.** The IPL family g preserves the abstract global/local strip tuple, including the abstract pilot, split monoids, Kummer unit data and localization. Beyond P, Q remains available as the original marked endpoint and its realization maps. The construction has not identified the two columns' ring structures or proved a descended native marking for the output. The SHE assertion in Remark 3.11.1 is not silently substituted for this latter step.

## STEP 3. Form the actual cQ3 presentation

**Statement.** A bounded admissible output family has a corrected determinant and a category-pair presentation in the native column.

**Source.** III Theorem 3.11(i),(ii), pp.153–156; Remark 3.9.5(i)–(vi), Ob1–6, cQ3, pp.127–135, 141–142; FrI Definition 1.2(i), p.21, Definition 1.3(i), p.24, Proposition 2.1(i), p.44; FrII Example 5.6(iii),(iv), pp.63–65.

**Construction.** Realize the output data (a),(b),(c) of Theorem 3.11 in the column-1 packets as stipulated there. On the bounded, admissible domain of the hull construction, form H_B. For each component take its determinant with the source's weight, tensor by the inverse corresponding determinant of the structural lattice, and clear denominators/twists with a common positive M. This is the source's D_B=det^(⊗M)(H_B), characterized on a structural module tensored by a line L by the output L^M. Assemble the local objects, a global realified object and their localization isomorphisms. Form C_B and the forgetful U_B:C_B→C_q, as displayed in the diagram file.

**Why hypotheses apply.** This step is conditional on an admissible relatively compact family and, globally, the stipulated finite-support or formal-approximation conditions. It does **not** claim that Theorem 3.11 alone bounds the total union of all possible outputs, or that Corollary 3.12 has already proved finiteness. The unbounded whole-space case is not turned into a determinant line. Formal over-region systems are permitted by Remark 3.9.7(ii),(iii), pp.146–147. If one reconstructs a full strip from a pilot degree, the negative-sign condition of Remark 2.4.2(iv)–(vi), pp.90–91, must additionally hold; existence of an ambient line object does not impose this sign automatically.

The local Frobenioids satisfy the pullback axiom of FrI Definition 1.3(i)(c): the category of pullbacks over an object is equivalent to the base slice. Applying this to objects with structure polymorphisms gives the compatible local pullback systems described by cQ3, with the source's Galois/unit actions. The model split-Kummer Frobenioids carry the auxiliary Kummer data, not bare abstract groups. FrII Example 5.6 is used only for its localization-by-restriction construction; cQ3 itself specifies the local-global tuples. No categorical reconstruction theorem for an arbitrary poly-Frobenioid is being invoked.

FrI Proposition 2.1(i) applies because the categories are Frobenioids and M is a positive integer. It gives the degree-M naive Frobenius functor up to functor isomorphism. It does not give an inverse unless additional perfectness holds; no such hypothesis is made. Nondivisibility and tensor-root twists remain as in Ob4.

**Resulting typed datum.** D_B, C_B, C_q and U_B, with the native q_M in C_q. The equations for U_B are explicit: `U_B(X,[a])=X`, `U_B(f)=f`; the localization tuple is unchanged. The operation on hulls is generally enlargement, and C_B is not identified with C_q. This proves construction of the **span** `C_B→C_q←{q_M}`, not an identification of its two arms.

## STEP 4. Test a concrete native-q compatibility that does work

**Statement.** In a fixed native realization, the hull and corrected determinant respect tensoring by a native arithmetic line. This does not establish the required returned-pilot relation.

**Source.** Elementary consequence of III Remark 3.9.5(i),(ii), Ob3, pp.127, 131–133, applied in the native models; not an additional theorem attributed to the source.

**Construction.** Multiplication by a nonzero local scalar a is a bijection of the ambient local field packet and of its hulls, preserving containment. Thus `φ(aB)=aφ(B)`. Component determinant multiplicativity and the fixed structural-lattice correction give

`det^(⊗M)(H⊗L)=det^(⊗M)(H)⊗L^M`.

Local formulas assemble using the localization isomorphisms of L. Tensoring the maps a_v by the identity on L^M gives a functor from C_B to the upper category for D_B⊗L^M, commuting with the corresponding ambient tensor functor. Taking L=L_q uses the original native marking. Isomorphism choices are kept as choices, not made canonical.

**Why hypotheses apply.** These operations occur within the **same native arithmetic realization**, with L an actual arithmetic line and B in the domain of Step 3. A fractional local scalar is nonzero, and positive real scaling is used in the archimedean components. A global trivialization of L is not needed; its local trivializations glue by the given line-bundle transitions.

**Resulting typed datum.** A native-q tensor comparison of **two** upper-category presentations, one for D_B and one for D_B⊗L_q^M. It changes the distinguished determinant object. Therefore even this stronger compatibility than merely sharing P does not prove that the returned IPL pilot has the original native q marking in the **fixed** output presentation. No scalar argument is used to identify those two objects.

## STEP 5. Retain the cQ4 relations, without inventing inverses

**Why hypotheses apply.** The adjustment is within column 1 of the fixed lattice, not across an arbitrary field isomorphism. Theorem 3.11(ii) and Remark 3.8.2 provide the LGP version of the stated log-Kummer comparisons.
