# Degree calibration: result and acceptance boundary

**2026-10-03 — milestone B: LOCAL_EXISTENCE_TARGET_CORRECTED.**

**GAMMA_CALIBRATION_AND_BOUNDEDNESS_UNRESOLVED.**

The strongest new result is a source-model calculation, not a new bridge theorem: at the fixed arithmetic-line presentation of cQ3, free local coefficients construct structure morphisms for the native Q_M and the hull determinant D_H. Their local/global *object* tuple exists without requiring those structure maps to come from one global comparison. Consequently bare Hom-set nonemptiness is too weak a target for the unresolved Gamma_chi application.

## What was obtained

[LOCAL_EXISTENCE_TEST](LOCAL_EXISTENCE_TEST.md) checks FrI's actual equation

`n alpha + z = f^* beta + Div_B(u)`

and its composition law. In the split finite-place model, the choice `u=s(alpha-f^*beta), z=0` gives a linear morphism; with a base isomorphism it is an isomorphism. At archimedean arithmetic-line fibers, the ratio of the two ball radii supplies a scalar isomorphism and a compatible torsion-quotient system. This proves an ordinary lift of the native tuple in the unrestricted fixed-presentation cQ3 category, conditional on the actual hull/determinant objects stipulated at the start. It proves neither essential surjectivity on arbitrary cQ3 objects nor membership in its optional hull-approximant subcategory.

The cQ3 object definition supplies local objects with structure-map orbits, a global object, and localization isomorphisms of those objects. It does not supply a global arrow to D_H whose localizations are those structure maps. This is a correction to our earlier emphasis, not a defect attributed to the source. The source's additional IPL/SHE construction, rather than this unrestricted ordinary fiber, must govern the Gamma-induced comparison.

The rational diagnostic is proved exactly. Independent local multipliers give Q_v isomorphic to H_v with `mu(Q)=1/p > 1/p^2=mu(H)`. Their defect is `Delta=-log(p)`, slack `S=0`. A principal global multiplier p has total norm one but fails the real containment. This is not an IUT countermodel.

## Calibration and effectivity, separately

[GAMMA_DEGREE_CALCULATION](GAMMA_DEGREE_CALCULATION.md) retains the actual Gamma_chi and all coupled choices, and derives the ordinary identity

`b-a=Delta+S`

as well as its source-weighted direct-product version and its corrected determinant/Picard formulation divided by the common M. Delta is specified from the comparison's action, independently of the target bound. Positivity of S requires an actual effective/bounded comparison.

There are genuine positive calibration mechanisms in the sources: global coefficient divisors have degree zero; III Remark 3.9.6 preserves inter-place conversion ratios and global degree for the stated vertical log comparison; stronger arithmetic-Frobenioid hypotheses can force degree one. An ordered realified Picard-line isomorphism alone allows a positive rescaling. The sign reduction fixes orientation and does not fix that scale. These facts are applied in their stated scopes, without converting an arbitrary family of local coefficients into a principal global one.

For Gamma_chi the induced ordered Picard map is available from the accepted stage 11 construction. The candidate intrinsic defect is

`Delta_chi^Pic = (nu_rec rho_chi,* gamma_chi - nu_q)([Q_M])/M`.

The source-permitted bounded receiving realization rho_chi has not been instantiated, so this is a typed criterion, **not a computed numerical Gamma defect**. Pulling back a degree coordinate merely to make the defect zero would not identify the receiving arithmetic normalization. The effect of delta_1 on that realized determinant is likewise not computed from its abstract unit-automorphism type.

No effective residual class or admissible calibrated approximation family for Gamma_chi has been constructed. Thus the original inequality does not follow from this run. The mathematical realization boundary is the same as stage 11; the genuinely new result is that ordinary local existence should not be confused with that boundary. The source is not shown to lack the needed construction, and no essential flaw or prize-relevant result is established.
