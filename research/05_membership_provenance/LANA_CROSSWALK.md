# LANA exact-S equality versus source specialization

The pinned report §§9–10, pp.43–49, presents a nine-step η algorithm and leaves (9-1) unproved. Its source and target are `Rval` from the BPS value-group portion and `Rss` from log-shell volume data. Choosing suitable S supplies an integral structure and a pilot represented by the reconstructed action on S. The native η^q uses the diagonally embedded native q-region. Equation (9-1) asks for an exact equality of the two maps for some suitable S.

This is **DIFFERENTLY_TYPED** from the pre-sQ5 formal category/diagram relation. The latter retains unit/Galois and reconstruction data. Equality after Rss does not, alone, reconstruct those data. The report's §10.3 refers to a comparison before computing degrees, while §9.2 already defines Rss using equality of volume. These levels must be explicitly reconciled in a translation, rather than treating the words “before degrees” as a new type definition.

| LANA ↔ ELS / FORM | UNKNOWN each direction | No source-certified bidirectional comparison of the admissible choice spaces and equality types. |

**Strength test:** under a shared admissible-output dictionary and common normalized target, an attained exact-S η equality is a sufficient and potentially stronger demand than downset membership. The reverse is not a formal consequence of the definition of a downset. This conditional observation is not a proof of strict strength or inequivalence for actual IUT. It does not revive a global Step-6 lift: LANA's detailed image-factorization language was corrected in Stage 3.5 and remains corrected.

The smallest additional crosswalk task is to specify the admissible S-domain and the normalization-preserving map from it to the source's formal output families, including whether its image is attained, dense, or merely generates a downset. That task is distinct from proving either bridge.
