Its Lemma 4.1 concerns a closed scalar half-line. Its model does not instantiate the IUT full prime-strip, cQ3 local/global category pair or cQ4 log-Kummer relation. Consequently it tests an extracted qualitative package, not the structured B1 audited here. The missing comparison certificate still has to be independently established; merely naming cQ3/cQ4 does not supply it.

The independent rescaling used in its example is not automatically admissible for IUT3's normalized Rss/log-link system. A complete source comparison would additionally require the still-unverified native-q specialization, not just the existence of a normalized scalar line.

The primary IUT facts behind this assessment are substantive: AT3 Prop5.7; IUT3 Prop3.9; Rmk3.9.4(iii)–(vi); Ob9 and Ob9-1; cQ3/cQ4. They fix local measures, packet/procession weights, q-generator evaluation, log-link-compatible values, and retained formal structure. These are explicit mechanisms exceeding a scale-free abstraction. They do **not** by themselves certify that a particular native q-rigidification is an admissible Θ-output comparison.
