## The conditional criterion, allowing relations

For any already recovered finite presentation diagram, retain its actual endpoint collections Y_j and let `S_j=T_Bj(Y_j)`. A section is a family `a_j∈S_j` satisfying the following constraints, with the indicated ambient identifications:

| Recovered edge type | Required condition |
|---|---|
| Ordinary fixed-B arrow f:Y_i→Y_j | `T_B(f)(a_j)=a_i`. |
| Functional presentation transport τ:S_i→S_j | `τ(a_i)=a_j`. |
| Genuine source-derived orbit relation R_ij⊆S_i×S_j | `(a_i,a_j)∈R_ij`, with its actual representative/diagram witness retained if composition requires it. |
| Two supplied root presentations over a powered one | Their forward orbit images equal the same powered value. Do not infer that this supplies any additional root presentation. |

These conditions define a subset of `∏_j S_j`; its nonemptiness is the compatible-section question. They demand neither one exact region nor a universal limit object. For a relational path, an intermediate witness must exist. An endpoint relation formed by forgetting intermediate witnesses is not used to manufacture that witness later.

To be a native-q section, the relevant endpoint must retain the original `Q=(Hq,•,0,ι)` and its native q_M tuple, splitting s_q,v, unit realization, prime labels and localization. The actual IPL provenance of the candidate section must also be supplied. An arbitrary choice in T_B(q_M), even if possible, is not automatically W*.
