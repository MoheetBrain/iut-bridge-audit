# Native q is a fixed reference

**The asymmetry is explicit.** Corollary 3.12, IUT3 p.174, subjects the Θ-pilot's possible images to Ind1/Ind2/Ind3, while explicitly excluding the native q-pilot log-volume from those indeterminacies. Its scalar is `a_q=-|log(q)|`. The audit must not put `eta_q` in the same presumed orbit as `eta_anab,S` merely to make a symmetric diagram.

“Fixed” has three different scopes that must be recorded separately:

1. **Native reference:** the codomain arithmetic holomorphic structure and native q-pilot are held fixed. This is the condition in xi-e and Remark 3.12.2(ii)(eitw)–(fitw), pp.188–189.
2. **Representatives:** the same abstract prime-strip has multiple labeled presentations. Roots of unity, suitable isomorphisms, tensor-power versions and logarithmically related realizations may change representatives. A literal cross-ring equation between q elements is generally not the appropriate type of statement.
3. **Scalar:** the source's normalization preserves the distinguished native scalar. This does not make every intermediate region equal to the native q-region.

## Scale and sign are constrained

Use distinct notation: `Q_Tate,v` is the elliptic curve's Tate parameter, whereas `q_under_v=Q_Tate,v^(1/(2ell))` is the root used in IUT1 Example 3.2(iv), p.71, determined modulo `mu_2ell`. The latter determines the characteristic splitting/divisor N-monoid. Its valuation is fixed; roots of unity contribute zero. Using the unrooted parameter without the `1/(2ell)` factor would change the normalization.

Remark 3.9.4(iv), IUT3 p.124, gives an explicit local statement: the unit/log-Kummer operations avoid interference with `q_under_v^N` up to roots of unity, and exponent one has normalized log-volume `-log(q_under_v)`. This negative region log-volume is distinct from the positive `|log(q)|` convention in the corollary.

Under sQ3, comparison with a determinant raised to power M entails comparison with the **known** q-pilot power M. Ob3–Ob4, pp.131–134, retain the power, Frobenioid categories and localization functors. Degree normalization compensates for M. A genuine intermediate change of tensor degree is therefore recorded; it is not hidden by saying “nothing changes.” No free positive rescaling follows from these passages.

Under sQ4, native-ring/logarithmic presentations may differ. The designated semisimplified transports preserve normalized log-volume; arbitrary-region preservation is not claimed. The fixed arithmetic holomorphic structure in the 1-column supports precise log-volume equalities in the source's comparison [Remark 3.12.2(v), pp.193–195]. The multiradial 0-column output has the different upper-bound role. These roles must not be collapsed.

## What remains unverified

The source explicitly claims that the q condition survives the full cQ3/cQ4 loop. This audit identifies the actions, localizations, splitting and coricity supporting that assertion, but has not independently established the categorical descent from those data to xi-f. “NO native reference change” in the table is not a proof that every intermediate map fixes a q element. Elementwise transport, orientation and scale questions without a supplied common type are marked UNKNOWN or NOT_DEFINED.
