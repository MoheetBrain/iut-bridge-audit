# sQ4/cQ4: vertical comparison with retained units

**Result: PARTIAL, with the domain restrictions and compatibility mechanisms identified.** The output of sQ3 is built from additive log-shells, whereas the input full prime-strip has multiplicative unit data. Both are in the Θ-link's codomain column, but at different vertical realizations. The log-Kummer correspondence supplies the adjustment making their Frobenioid objects comparable [IUT3 Remark 3.9.5 Ob8, pp.137–138]. It is not a ring isomorphism across the Θ-link.

At a finite bad place the relevant structures include the local `G_v`, its rigidification as a quotient of an isomorph of `Pi_v`, vertically coric étale units `O^units,mu(G_v)`, the Frobenius-like units at label m, and the splitting monoid containing the q powers up to torsion. These fields must remain distinguished. The Frobenius-like units, value part and splitting use the **same** underlying multiplicative structure at m [Ob7, pp.135–137]. Replacing them by an unrelated coric unit object would discard precisely the compatibility under examination.

cQ4 calls compatibility tautological because the vertical adjustment is **constructed using** the log-Kummer correspondence, whose suitable Galois and étale-unit isomorphisms are vertically coric [Remark 3.9.5(ix), p.142, referring to Theorem 1.5(i)]. The alternative statement with Frobenius-like units requires allowing arbitrary log-link iterates. “Tautological” here identifies a construction-level compatibility; it does not certify that arbitrary composites are identity maps or that every relevant formal descent has been checked by this audit.

## Actual arrows and iteration

Proposition 3.5(ii), pp.104–106, and Theorem 3.11(ii), pp.155–156, distinguish:

- Kummer isomorphisms at specified realizations.
- Positive log iterates **on their domains of definition**, precomposed with the relevant maps.
- Finite-place containment in the coric module/log-shell container.
- Archimedean surjections from specified ball subsets onto the unit subsets where the requisite iteration is defined.

The **totality** of those precomposites forms the correspondence invariant under translating the Z-indexed column. A single Kummer isomorphism is not log-compatible in the required sense. Translation of the labeled family is not a group action of Ind3 on arbitrary regions, choices S or η-maps. The finite inclusions and archimedean surjections have not become invertible.

A conservative encoding is the diagram of tagged realizations `(m, object, G_v, units, splitting, local-global data)` with the permitted arrows just described. A **formal zigzag relation** is generated by matching endpoints of these arrows and the source comparison isomorphisms, keeping all tags and compatibility diagrams. If one subsequently takes the equivalence closure on tagged presentations, that is an audit encoding of formal iteration indeterminacy, not a claim that each log arrow has an inverse function or that the source supplied a categorical localization with a proved universal property. The latter specification remains UNKNOWN and is not required merely for its own sake. The necessary check is compatibility of the actual permitted iteration diagram with q-constrained comparison and evaluation; a universal localization or group action need not be supplied. In particular, there is no justification for identifying two arbitrary regions merely because their vertical labels differ.

## Scalar compatibility is narrower than region compatibility

Remark 3.9.4(iii)–(vi), pp.122–126 constructs semisimplified transports using compact ample subsets where log is injective, then proves independence of that auxiliary subset in the local construction. Tensor-packet and archimedean analogues are included. Remark 3.9.6, p.145, explicitly limits the relevant region-level compatibility: it is not volume preservation for every measurable region.

At bad places Remark 3.9.4(iv), p.124 describes the unit/log operations as avoiding interference with the splitting `q^N`, up to roots of unity. The normalized log-volume of the image of `O_k` under exponent one is `-log(q)`. This is a concrete contribution to native-q rigidity. It does not by itself show that the whole output formal quotient has a scalar representative equal to the native pilot.
