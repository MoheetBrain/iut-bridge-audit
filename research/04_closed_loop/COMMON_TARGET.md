# Common target: what is reconstructed and what remains

**PARTIAL.** The IUT sources supply considerably more than an unnamed “copy of R.” They give a local semisimplification, its operations, normalized log transport, weights and root conventions. A complete canonical transport identifying that construction with both of LANA's global η targets has not yet been certified.

## Three related, distinct types

LANA's `Rval` is the C/value-group part of the BPS, described as an oriented real line with local pilots and a resulting distinguished pilot [§4.1–4.2, pp.24–26; §9.2–9.3, p.46]. Its η maps are described as pointed real-vector-space isomorphisms. This is the input line, not a newly scaled output chosen to force equality.

IUT3 Remark 3.9.4(i), pp.120–122, fixes an ample measure space `(M,mu)` and defines `Fn(M)` as the nonnegative finite-image Borel functions with relatively compact positive support. Equal integral gives an explicit monoid quotient `Rss_mu(M)`, canonically identified by integration with `R_{>=0}`. The monoid has its positive real scalar action; groupification supplies an ordered one-dimensional real vector space. Characteristic functions of relatively compact regions embed the relevant region computations in this formalism. This resolves a substantial local part of U06.

On positive classes, **normalized log-volume** is a positive normalization constant times `log(integral)`. Zero maps to `-infinity`; the finite real coordinate requires positive finite integral. Log-volume and the additive monoid's groupification must not be called the same linear construction without specifying the transported operations: `log(a+b)` is not `log(a)+log(b)`.

LANA §9.2 describes its `Rss` directly as adelic measurable regions in the large container modulo equal volume, and calls it a real vector space. The quotation's domain restrictions, vector operations and global identification with the IUT Fn construction are compressed. We have not supplied missing operations by fiat. The local IUT construction reduces the ambiguity; it does not automatically finish the crosswalk.

## Canonical transports actually supplied

At finite places, take the additive Haar measure with `mu_k(O_k)=1`. Compact ample subsets of units on which log is injective induce compatible Rss isomorphisms among `k`, the unit portion, its logarithmic image and k again; the resulting local comparison is independent of the auxiliary subset [Remark 3.9.4(iii), pp.122–124]. These are canonical semisimplified transports at their stated scope, not total measure-preserving maps on every region.

Finite extensions are compared through the prescribed tensor powers of Rss; tensor packets use the corresponding degree products [iii,v, pp.123–125]. Archimedean radial/angular constructions have their own measures and exponential-covering formulation [vi, pp.125–126]. These are the named source transports available for reconciling vertical realizations. The Θ-link itself is not a canonical ring/measure identification.

## Pilot, root and sign

Write `Q_Tate,v` for the original Tate parameter. IUT1 Example 3.2(iv), p.71 defines the relevant root `q_under_v=Q_Tate,v^(1/(2ell))` modulo `mu_2ell`; its divisor determines the characteristic splitting N-monoid. This is a prescribed normalization, not an arbitrary choice of positive scale. LANA's q typography must be reconciled with this convention when constructing the full crosswalk.

IUT3 Remark 3.9.4(iv), p.124 sends exponent one of the splitting to the normalized log-volume `-log(q_under_v)`. Globally the corollary uses `a_q=-|log(q)|`, with positive absolute-value degree notation. The native q-region and the Θ-action region `1·S` must be defined separately. They cannot be declared to be the same distinguished target element merely because each is called a pilot.

The diagonal tensor embedding uses the distinguished unit 1 in the other factors [IUT3 Proposition 3.3(ii), p.100]. The identification of label 0 with the diagonal `⟨F_ell^{⋇}⟩` label is referenced in Remark 3.3.2, p.101 and IUT2 Corollary 4.10(i), pp.158–159, through Corollaries 4.5(iii) and 4.6(iii). This identifies the source route; a complete equivalence with every “final label” convention in LANA's procession notation remains to be expanded. A diagonal copy is not an independently chosen q-region at each label.

## Packet and procession normalization

Remark 3.1.1, IUT3 pp.94–97, specifies positive rational local/tensor weights. With `r_v=[K_v:Fmod,v]` and `d_v=[Fmod,v:Q_vQ]`, the normalized local coefficient is `1/(r_v sum_{w|vQ} d_w)`. Tensor components use the inverse product of the corresponding r's and the sum of products of base degrees. These are applied to the source component log-volumes, not arbitrary weights chosen for the audit.

The replication construction using finite sets E in Remark 3.1.1(iv) explains the weighted log sum for product regions. Its behavior on completely arbitrary regions can include zero or infinite measure; it is not ordinary Haar measure on an unrestricted class. Proposition 3.9 fixes the structural O normalization, prime-multiplication/radial shift laws and global summation. Procession capsules receive the **equal average** of Proposition 3.9(i)–(ii) and Remark 3.9.3, p.120. Packet weights and equal capsule averaging are different operations.

## How an honest equality test would be typed

Fix the native data D, an oriented domain V, and one target L constructed independently of the two target pilots. Construct source-certified transports `iota_q:Rss_q -> L` and `iota_S:Rss_S -> L` using the specified local/global normalized comparison. Then compare

`iota_q ∘ eta_q = iota_S ∘ eta_anab,S : V -> L`.

This is a **typing prescription**, not an asserted new canonical construction. Local transports above are known; their complete global agreement with LANA's η definitions and admissibility remains PARTIAL. A transport defined by sending one disputed pilot to the other would trivialize the target and is prohibited. Once valid common linear types and nonzero domain pilot are fixed, equality on that pilot determines equality of linear maps; verifying the disputed pilot equality is still the task, not a convention adopted here.
