Existing attacks stop at lower levels of faithfulness.

Higuchi-style logical countermodels establish, at most, that certain
qualitative abstractions do not imply the desired numerical conclusion.

Pajo-style rescaling arguments establish, at most, that scale-free
common-container abstractions do not imply numerical inequalities.

Neither by itself establishes:

    M ⊨ actual relevant IUT hypotheses
    but
    η_q ∉ O_anab(D).

Conversely, Mochizuki's reply to Scholze–Stix argues that faithful IUT
contains structure omitted by simplified linear/scaling models.

Therefore the next project cannot simply build a more complicated toy model.

It must first answer:

    WHAT EXACTLY IS Adm(D)?

and:

    WHAT EXACTLY CAN EACH ADMISSIBLE CHOICE CHANGE?

Only then can we search for an invariant, obstruction, or witness.
